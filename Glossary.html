Glossary

  1. Navgrah:
    2. The term used for the collection of the 9 planets in vedic astrology.
  2. Grahs:
    3. Simply, a planet.
  3. Surya:
    4. Sun, the star of our solar system.
  4. Tamas:
    5. Lethargy, lack of energy to work.
  5. Simh:
    6. The 5th sign of the zodiac, Leo.
  6. Ahankar:
    7. Unhealthy ego, involving in believing oneself as superior from others.
  7. Gayatri Mantra:
    8. A powerful mantra in hinduism, which involves saluting the Sun god and asking for a clear intellect
    ॐ भूर्भुवः स्वः तत्सवितुर्वरेण्यं भर्गो देवस्य धीमहि धियो यो नः प्रचोदयात्
  8. Mantra:
    9. A sacred sound or vibration to invoke or salute a deity, often used for protection and various other things.
  9. Arghya:
    10. An offering to a deity, out of gratitude and respect, often of water.
