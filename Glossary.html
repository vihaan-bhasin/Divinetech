Glossary about hard words of Sun in Astrology

  1. Divakar:
    2. A term used for giving respect to the Sun, as the creator of day.
  2. Daanvir:
    3. Someone who donates a lot, it is used for Surya, to respect his donation of light and heat to the Earth.
  3. R̥śi :
    4. A sage.
  4. Tejas:
    5. Radiance, often used for gods and sages. Tejas is created through austerity.
  5. Tamas:
    6. Lethargy, lack of energy to work.
  6. Navgrah:
    7. The term used for the collection of the 9 planets in vedic astrology.
  7. Grahs:
    8. Simply, a planet.
  8. Surya:
    9. Sun, the star of our solar system.
  9. Simh:
    10. The 5th sign of the zodiac, Leo.
  10. Ahankar:
    11. Unhealthy ego, involving in believing oneself as superior from others.
  11. Gayatri Mantra:
    12. A powerful mantra in hinduism, which involves saluting the Sun god and asking for a clear intellect
    ॐ भूर्भुवः स्वः तत्सवितुर्वरेण्यं भर्गो देवस्य धीमहि धियो यो नः प्रचोदयात्
  12. Mantra:
    13. A sacred sound or vibration to invoke or salute a deity, often used for protection and various other things.
  13. Arghya:
    14. An offering to a deity, out of gratitude and respect, often of water.
  14. Karma:
    15. Our deeds and actions, karma may be good or bad, and based on these deeds, we get the karmphal, or 'the results of actions'.
