1.2.2: Planets in detail

Now, let us see what impact does each planet do on our body and what each planet represents:

Sun

Sun is considered the representation of Soul, Identity and fame in society in vedic astrology. In the royal court of Navgrahs . He is the respected king which is the greatest of all the grahs.
Surya is considered the destroyer of tamas by providing his heat to the Earth. He is the ruler of Simh rashi, which depicts a lion.
Out of all grahs, most authoratative planet is Surya, because he is the king. No planet can ever offer the same amount of respect and glory to a native as much as Sun.
