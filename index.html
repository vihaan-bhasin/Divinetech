Signs of a troubled Sun:

  1. Lack of self-respect and boundaries:
    2.  A person with a troubled sun may struggle to keep his self-respect and boundaries, and might struggle to reject others.
  2. Fight with father:
    3.  Native with a weak Sun may have a troubled relation with his father.
  3. Lack of purpose:
    4.  If one cannot find the direction or purpose in his life, then his Sun may be troubled.

NOTE:

One thing which is worth observing is that when Sun or any other planet is weak in the birth chart,
it does NOT necessarily mean that it will show bad results behaviorally also. A person with a weak Sun
also may have self-respect and boundaries. A person with a strong sun also might struggle with boundaries.
This happens merely because of karma of our past lives, we get the position of planets based on our past life karms,
which HELP us in advancing ahead, not PREDICT our future, so if these positions happen by our own karmas, then our karm can help change them also.
These positions help in advancing ahead and not simply determine our future because, astrology can always show the possibilities but never with full certainty.
It shows possibilities of a thing happening, but never guarantess it, and astrology itself is used to see our own weaknesses and strengths
So using this wonderful ability of astrology, by which we see strengths and weaknesses, each individual shall find the path of advancement suitable to them.

Moon

Characteristics :

The Moon is a benefic and a very important planet in vedic astrology. In the royal court of navgrahs, he is considered the Queen.
Here, the moon is referred as 'he' and 'queen' at the same time, this is because the deity of moon is masculine, but the attributes of it is feminine, such as it represents emotions.

NOTE:

here, it is not intended to say that men do not have emotions, so please read this text with an open mind and do not arrive at this conclusion!

It represents the mind, our emotions, water and mother. It is white in color, it is very important grah because in a philosophy of hinduism, its mentioned man ev manushyāṇām kāraṇam bandhmokshayo. It directly means that both bondage and freedom are caused by the mind and its perspectives. if one thinks the Samsar as a place which should be left ASAP, then he really is bound to it .
Although, if the same guy lives freely and enjoys it, then he is not bound.
