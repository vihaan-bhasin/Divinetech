Moon

Characteristics :

The Moon is a benefic and a very important planet in vedic astrology. In the royal court of navgrahs, he is considered the Queen.
Here, the moon is referred as 'he' and 'queen' at the same time, this is because the deity of moon is masculine, but the attributes of it is feminine, such as it represents emotions.

NOTE:

here, it is not intended to say that men do not have emotions, so please read this text with an open mind and do not arrive at this conclusion!

It represents the mind, our emotions, water and mother. It is white in color, it is very important grah because in a philosophy of hinduism, its mentioned man ev manushyāṇām kāraṇam bandhmokshayo. It directly means that both bondage and freedom are caused by the mind and its perspectives. if one thinks the Samsar as a place which should be left ASAP, then he really is bound to it .
Although, if the same guy lives freely and enjoys it, then he is not bound.
